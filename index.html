How do I get my export?
  1. In Instagram open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information (called Download your information in some versions).
  2. Create an export, choose Some of your information, and tick only Followers and following.
  3. Pick Download to device, date range All time, format JSON.
  4. When Instagram emails you, download the ZIP and drop it above. No login needed here.

    Backup & data

    Backup

    Save your Follow Check work to a file on this device: tags, Review Mode progress, the Unfollow Queue and your session target. Restore it later, or in another browser. The backup contains the usernames you tagged or queued, but none of your Instagram export. It is never uploaded, so keep the file somewhere private.

    Saved data in this browser

    • Clear review progress Deletes every Keep / Ignore / Unavailable tag and your Review Mode position. The Unfollow Queue is not changed.
    • Clear Unfollow Queue progress Puts every queued account back to Remaining (clears Done and Skipped) and forgets your place. The accounts stay in the queue.
    • Clear Unfollow Queue Removes every account from the Unfollow Queue, with its progress. Tags are not changed.

    Danger zone

    Clear all Follow Check saved data deletes all tags, review progress, the Unfollow Queue and session settings from this browser. It can't be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it back.

    These options only affect data this page saved in this browser. Follow Check never changes or deletes your Instagram ZIP or any other file.

    Unfollow Queue

    Follow Check never unfollows anyone and can't see Instagram. Open the profile, press Unfollow on Instagram yourself, then press Mark done here. “Done” only records what you told Follow Check.
